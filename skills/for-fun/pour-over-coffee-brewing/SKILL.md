# ☕ Master Pour-Over Coffee Brewing

A comprehensive guide to calibrating equipment, executing precision pour-over frameworks, and dialing in extraction parameters for exceptional coffee.

---

## ⚙️ Equipment Calibration (Timemore S3 Chestnut Grinder)

* **Top-Adjustment Collar**: External collar marked from **0.0 to 9.0**.
* **Dial Math**: 1 full number = 10 clicks (0.1 per click). 1 full rotation (360°) = 90 clicks.
* **Finding Zero**: Turn collar clockwise until it stops gently (`0.0`).
* **Safety Protocol**: **Never** store or travel with the dial locked at `0.0`. Loosen to **6.0+** before storing to prevent burr damage.

## ☕ Brewing Frameworks

### 1. Framework A: The 3-Pour Method (Balanced & Efficient)
* **Best For**: High clarity, thermal consistency, balanced acidity/sweetness.
* **Grind**: **5.5 – 6.5** | **Temp**: **84°C – 88°C** | **Ratio**: **1:15** (20g coffee to 300g water)
* **Schedule**:
  * **0:00**: Pour **100g** (Bloom + gentle swirl, rest 45s)
  * **0:45**: Pour **100g** (Steady center pour, total 200g)
  * **1:30**: Pour **100g** (Final gentle top-up, total 300g)
  * **2:45**: Target finish drawdown

### 2. Framework B: The Adjusted 4:6 Method (Sweetness & Body Focus)
* **Best For**: Maximum natural sweetness and smooth body (e.g., *Nicaragua Organic*).
* **Grind**: **7.5 – 8.5** (Coarse) | **Temp**: **83°C – 86°C** | **Ratio**: **1:15** (20g coffee to 300g water)
* **Schedule (45s intervals)**:
  * **Phase 1 (Sweetness 40%)**: `0:00` Pour **50g** (Bloom) ➔ `0:45` Pour **70g** (Total 120g)
  * **Phase 2 (Smooth Body 60%)**: `1:30` Pour **90g** (Total 210g) ➔ `2:15` Pour **90g** (Total 300g)
  * **3:00 – 3:45**: Target finish drawdown

## 🎯 Dialing-In Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Root Cause | Action / Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dry, bitter aftertaste (Astringency)** | Fines clogging flow during later pours | Coarsen grind by **+0.5** (e.g. 8.0 ➔ 8.5); pour gently. |
| **Bitter first sip + fast drawdown (<2:30)** | Micro-channeling from fine grind | Coarsen grind slightly (**+0.3**) to balance bed resistance. |
| **Mild, thin, or hollow taste** | Ratio too diluted for bean solubility | Tighten ratio from 1:15 to **1:14** (20g coffee to 280g water). |

## 🌡️ Temperature Selection Guide

* **88°C**: High-altitude, dense washed beans (e.g., *Nicaragua Organic*).
* **85°C ("Bingo Temp")**: Medium-dark roasts (e.g., *Ethiopia Pat Dark*, *Kenya Maasi Gold*).
* **80°C – 84°C**: Very dark, low-acidity blends (e.g., *Musashino Mori*, *Roar Wolf*).
