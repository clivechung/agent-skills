# ⚙️ Grinder Calibration Guide: Timemore S3 Chestnut Manual Grinder

The Timemore S3 Chestnut Manual Grinder features an external top-adjustment dial designed for precise, repeatable grind size control.

---

## 1. Dial Anatomy & Mechanics

* **Adjustment Mechanism**: External top-adjustment collar.
* **Collar Markings**: Marked from **0.0 to 9.0**.
* **Click Calculations**:
  * **1 full number** (e.g., 8.0 to 9.0) = **10 clicks** (0.1 per click).
  * **1 full 360° rotation** = **90 clicks**.

---

## 2. Zero Point & Safety Protocols

> [!CAUTION]
> **Protect Your Burrs**: Never store or transport the grinder with the dial locked at `0.0`. Direct metal-on-metal contact will dull or chip the steel burrs.

1. **Finding True Zero**:
   * Gently turn the top collar clockwise until it stops turning. Do not force it.
   * This resting point is your zero reference (`0.0`).
2. **Storage & Travel Rule**:
   * Before packing away or storing the grinder, always loosen the collar to a coarse setting (e.g., **6.0+**).
