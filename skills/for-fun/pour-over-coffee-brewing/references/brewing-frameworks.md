# ☕ Brewing Frameworks Reference

Detailed recipes and step-by-step pour schedules for pour-over coffee.

---

## Framework A: The 3-Pour Method (Balanced & Efficient)

Foundational "goldilocks" method for high flavor clarity, thermal consistency, and balanced extraction.

* **Target Profile**: High clarity, balanced acidity and sweetness, medium body.
* **Grind Setting (Timemore S3)**: **5.5 to 6.5**
* **Water Temperature**: **84°C – 88°C**
* **Brew Ratio**: **1:15** (20g coffee to 300g water)

### Pacing Schedule

| Timestamp | Action | Target Weight | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00** | Pour **100g** | 100g | Gentle bloom with a swirl; rest until 0:45 |
| **0:45** | Pour **100g** | 200g | Steady center pour |
| **1:30** | Pour **100g** | 300g | Final gentle top-up pour |
| **2:45** | Drawdown Finish | 300g | Bed should drain completely |

---

## Framework B: The Adjusted 4:6 Method (Sweetness & Smooth Body Focus)

Mathematically splits the brew into distinct flavor and strength phases for maximum sweetness and a smooth body.

* **Target Profile**: Enhanced natural sweetness, clean acidity, smooth texture.
* **Grind Setting (Timemore S3)**: **7.5 to 8.5** (Coarse)
* **Water Temperature**: **83°C – 86°C**
* **Brew Ratio**: **1:15** (20g coffee to 300g water)

### Pacing Schedule (45-second intervals)

| Phase | Timestamp | Action | Target Weight | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Sweetness (40%)** | **0:00** | Pour **50g** | 50g | Small bloom highlights natural sweetness |
| | **0:45** | Pour **70g** | 120g | Completes flavor balance phase |
| **Phase 2: Strength & Body (60%)** | **1:30** | Pour **90g** | 210g | First strength pour; fewer pours give lighter, smoother texture |
| | **2:15** | Pour **90g** | 300g | Final strength pour |
| **Finish** | **3:00 – 3:45** | Drawdown Finish | 300g | Bed drains completely |
